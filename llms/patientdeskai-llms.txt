# Patientdesk.ai

> Patientdesk.ai is the AI booking system for dental practices. It automatically handles patient calls, appointment scheduling, real-time insurance verification, payment collection, and follow-ups 24/7 — helping dental clinics increase revenue and never miss a patient call again.

Patientdesk AI, Inc. is a California-based company backed by Y Combinator. The product is an AI receptionist that integrates with major practice management systems (Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, and more) to book appointments directly, verify insurance in real-time during calls, and collect outstanding payments automatically.

Key capabilities:
- 24/7 AI phone receptionist for dental practices
- Real-time insurance verification during patient calls
- Automated payment collection and debt follow-up
- Direct PMS integration (Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Curve Dental, etc.)
- Lead generation and patient acquisition
- Multi-language support
- Month-to-month contracts with refundable setup fee
- HIPAA compliant with SOC 2 security standards
- No medical triage — follows clinic guidelines only
